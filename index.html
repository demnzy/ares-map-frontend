The problem

Mars data is everywhere. A map for the person walking is not.

NASA has years of data from rovers and orbiters, but existing tools are built for global and regional views. An astronaut on a four-hour walk needs to know something closer: what will conditions be like at this spot, at this hour, along this path?

Huge temperature swings

From about -100°C before dawn to +20°C at noon. That strains suit batteries and seals.

Dust that cuts visibility

Wind can lift dust quickly, making it easy to lose your bearings far from the habitat.

Radiation with no shield

Mars has no global magnetic field, so exposed ridgelines carry the highest dose.

Data layers

Four hazards, one map.

Behind the scenes, all four are packed into one image. Each pixel of the map stores one score per hazard, from 0 (safe) to 1 (dangerous), in its red, green, blue and alpha channels.

Switch each layer on or off to see what it adds to the picture.

RRed channel0 warm, 1 freezing

Thermal

Finds dangerously cold spots, such as shadowed crater walls.

GGreen channel0 clear, 1 heavy dust

Dust

Shows where wind-blown dust is likely to build up and cut visibility.

BBlue channel0 sheltered, 1 exposed

Radiation

Higher on open ridges, lower in deep hollows and behind boulders.

AAlpha channel0 gentle, 1 blocked

Slope

Flags ground steeper than 15°, where a pressurized suit can tip over.

How the map reads the ground

Same map, three very different spots.

Every spot on the map gets a score for each hazard. Here is how the route planner reads three of them.

Safe to walk

Sunlit crater floor

Flat, warm ground in open sun.

  • RThermalLow
  • GDustLow
  • BRadiationLow
  • ASlopeLow

Route planner: an easy, low-cost path. No warning is shown.

Blocked

Shadowed delta cliff

A steep wall in permanent shade.

  • RThermalHigh
  • GDustMedium
  • BRadiationMedium
  • ASlopeHigh

Route planner: impassable. The map shows a cold warning with slope hatching.

Risky

Exposed ridge top

Open, windy ground high above the crater.

  • RThermalLow
  • GDustHigh
  • BRadiationHigh
  • ASlopeLow

Route planner: walkable but costly, so it avoids it. The map tints it red and ochre.
