1. Problem Statement & Target Site

Orbital maps are static 2D rasters. Surface EVAs face dynamic 3D hazards.

Existing planetary visualization tools such as JPL Mars Trek serve macro-scale orbital science on static 2D projections. Crew executing surface Extravehicular Activities (EVAs) require localized 3D topography coupled with hour-by-hour environmental modeling—where solar elevation angle, local slope gradients, horizon sky-view occlusion, and atmospheric dust dynamically dictate traversal safety.

Why Jezero Crater (Western Delta): Selected for having the richest co-located multi-instrument dataset on Mars. Our pipeline ingests sub-meter HiRISE stereophotogrammetry (PSP_003798_1985 / DTEEC_045994_1985_046060_1985_U01, spanning -2,614.1 m to -2,449.7 m elevation) alongside a 45 × 45 km regional DEM across the lacustrine delta fan and Perseverance meteorology records.

Diurnal thermal extremes (−92.0°C to +18.6°C)

Across 25 MEDA reference sols (Sols 1–1321), ground temperature drops to −92.03°C (median −60.10°C) while air temperature spans −87.51°C to +18.59°C. Shadowed scarps and katabatic basins drive suit heater load spikes.

Dust optical depth & drift (τ = 0.180 – 2.508)

Derived from MEDA CAL_TIRS downward longwave atmospheric emissivity and seasonal perihelion dust loading (median τ = 0.628), coupled with DER_WS winds (0.30–8.97 m/s) that advect sand across low basins.

Unshielded ionizing dose (9.65 – 12.98 µGy/hr)

With no global magnetic dipole and thin CO₂ column pressure (622.85–807.61 Pa in DER_PS), Curiosity RAD observations track persistent galactic cosmic ray flux peaking on ridges with high Sky-View Factor.

2. System Architecture & Intermediary Protocol

Four-stage pipeline from NASA PDS archives to 3D shader unpacking.

The 3D rendering engine never parses raw planetary archives directly. Instead, a decoupled Python FastAPI backend executes PyTorch temporal inference, fuses predicted environmental scalars with cached HiRISE topographic derivatives, and streams a single 32-bit RGBA PNG texture over REST.

01 · EPHEMERIS

Keplerian Ls & 9D Harmonic Features

Solves Kepler's equation (P = 668.5991 sols, e = 0.0934) to map target Sol to Solar Longitude Ls, building a 9-dim vector of 1st/2nd diurnal and seasonal sin/cos modes plus mission drift (sol / 1340).

02 · PYTORCH ML

MarsEnvMLP Residual Regressor

Pre-norm residual network (9 → 128 → 2×ResBlock(128, LayerNorm, SiLU, Dropout 0.05) → 64 → 7) outputting telemetry scalars plus unit-circle (sin, cos) wind components recombined via atan2 in 2.71 ms p50.

03 · SYNTHESIZER

32-Bit RGBA Mask Bridge

JezeroHazardSynthesizer combines predicted scalars with cached HiRISE elevation gradients (dx, dy), slope angles, and dual-radius Sky-View Factor (SVF) into a single packed RGBA PNG returned via POST /api/forecast.

04 · 3D ENGINE

WebGL 2 Terrain & Shader Unpack

The 3D simulator (built separately in Godot 4 Compatibility / WebGL 2) displaces static 4096×4096 16-bit heightmaps, unpacks RGBA channels in ares_terrain.gdshader, binds τ to volumetric fog, and rotates sunlight to LMST.

4. Packed 32-Bit RGBA Intermediary Texture (dynamic_hazard_mask.png)

Four physical hazard fields encoded into one 32-bit RGBA buffer.

Each pixel in dynamic_hazard_mask.png (configurable 64×64 to 2048×2048) stores four normalized 8-bit scalars [0.0 – 1.0] derived in backend/synthesizer.py. Packing all layers into one PNG lets the 3D shader sample all hazards in a single texture fetch.

RChannel R · 8-bit [0.0–1.0]0 warm, 1 cryogenic cold stress

Thermal Cold Stress

Combines predicted ground temperature with solar hill-shading from DTM gradients (dx, dy), cold-air pooling in low basins (1 − elev_norm), and radiative cooling via Sky-View Factor (SVF).

cold = clip((15 - Tg)/110, 0.05, 1)
topo = 0.45*(1-hs) + 0.35*(1-z) + 0.20*svf
R = clip(cold * (0.35 + 0.65*topo), 0, 1)
GChannel G · 8-bit [0.0–1.0]0 clear, 1 dense drift & haze

Dust & Aeolian Drift

Generates an anisotropic 3-octave wavefield rotated along predicted wind bearing θw, stretched by wind speed (1 + v/8), and concentrated inside low-elevation basins weighted by optical depth τ.

tau_s = clip((tau - 0.15)/1.45, 0.08, 1)
basin = 0.55 + 0.45*(1 - elev_norm)
G = clip(wave(u, v, θw) * basin * tau_s)
BChannel B · 8-bit [0.0–1.0]0 sheltered, 1 peak exposure

Ionizing Radiation

Scales predicted RAD dose rate by topographic exposure and dual-radius (N/32, N/10) horizon occlusion SVF ∈ [0.45, 1.0], capturing shelter behind steep crater walls versus open ridges.

rad_s = clip((rad - 9.0)/5.0, 0.25, 1)
exp = 0.60*elev_norm + 0.40*svf
B = clip((0.15+0.85*exp)*(0.45+0.55*rad_s))
AChannel A · 8-bit [0.0–1.0]0 flat, 1 impassable (≥15°)

Slope Traversability

Computed directly from HiRISE DTM elevation gradients at 1.01 m/px native spacing. Values hitting 1.0 mark slopes ≥15° (1.21% at 256px, 1.48% at 1024px) where pressurized suits risk tipping.

dy, dx = np.gradient(elev_m, cell_m)
slope_deg = deg(arctan(hypot(dx, dy)))
A = clip(slope_deg / 15.0, 0.0, 1.0)

5. Terrain Reconstruction & Synthesized Layer Outputs

Jezero Crater 3D terrain & synthesized RGBA hazard channels.

Visual outputs generated directly from our NASA HiRISE DTM (4,135.6 m × 4,135.6 m), 45 × 45 km regional DEM, and JezeroHazardSynthesizer (1024×1024) that feed the external 3D simulator engine.

Real DTM Elevation: Displaced directly from NASA HiRISE PDS .IMG rasters and 16-bit 4096×4096 heightmaps.

Single-Buffer Encoding: All four channels (R, G, B, A) pack into one PNG buffer for single-fetch shader sampling.

Engine-Agnostic Output: Consumed identically by Godot 4 WebGL 2 shaders, Blender/glTF loaders, and route planners.

6. 3D Terrain Asset Pipeline & Engine Contract

Built for WebGL 2 shader splatmapping and lossless tile streaming.

4096²

16-Bit Heightmaps & 9×9 Ortho Tiles

process_hirise.py and slice_ortho_chunks.py convert raw HiRISE .IMG DTMs and .JP2 orthoimagery into 16-bit grayscale heightmaps plus 81 lossless RGB PNG chunks (chunk_01..81) with exact UV, kilometer, and scene-meter manifest bounds.

.GLB / .OBJ

Displaced Landscape Mesh Exporter

export_terrain_mesh.py compiles binary glTF 2.0 (.glb) and Wavefront (.obj + .mtl) terrain meshes with area-weighted smooth vertex normals for both the 4.14 km HiRISE Western Delta and 45 km regional Jezero Crater landscapes.

WebGL 2

Godot 4 Compatibility & COOP/COEP

Specified for Godot 4 Compatibility renderer (OpenGL ES 3.0 / WebGL 2.0) with Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin and Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp headers. ares_terrain.gdshader unpacks RGBA channels and maps τ to volumetric fog.
